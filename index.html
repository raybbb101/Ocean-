How to play

  • 🏝️ Start at the Home Dock. Dive, collect, come back and sell at the shop.
  • 🫧 Oxygen drains faster the deeper you are. Surface anywhere to refill.
  • ⚖️ The deeper you go, the heavier you get — you sink and swim slower. Cargo adds weight too.
  • 🧰 Your bag has a weight limit. Black out and you lose your cargo!
  • 🧪 Going below your suit's safe depth hurts. Upgrade the suit to go deeper.
  • 🔱 Hostile creatures (jellies, barracudas, sharks, anglerfish…) can be fought off with the harpoon. Some drop trophies.
  • ⚓ Surfacing next to a buoy unlocks a boat stop for fast travel.
  • 📖 Fill the Journal by discovering every species and treasure.

PC

Move: WASD/Arrows · Grab: E/Space · Harpoon: Mouse or F · Shop/Boat: B · Menu: Esc

Mobile

Drag the left side to swim · ✋ grab · 🔱 harpoon (auto-aims) · SHOP/BOAT button appears at the surface.

Co-op

One player presses Host Co-op and shares the 5-letter code. Up to 3 friends enter it and press Join. Everyone keeps their own coins and upgrades.

📲 Install Deep Dive Explorers

Install the game like an app: it gets its own icon, opens full-screen and works offline (accounts and co-op need internet).

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

Tap the Share button ⬆️, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Windows / Mac / Linux (Chrome or Edge)

Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the ⋮ menu, then Install Deep Dive Explorers.
